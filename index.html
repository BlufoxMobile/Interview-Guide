Interview Guide

Complete this guide before hiring anyone in the market. The submission will be forwarded to the director, the DM, the store manager, and the hiring director.

1 Interview Basics ▼
2 Introduction to the Interview ▼
Script prompt: Blufox Mobile is a branded partner of Xfinity. We sell TV, Internet, Home Phone, Home Security, and Cell Phones. Started in Tomball, TX in 2017 — now the largest branded partner of Xfinity. We have ____ stores nationally and ____ stores in this region.
Schedule expectation: Full-time, two shifts available (9:30 AM – 6 PM or 12 PM – 8:30 PM). Open availability required. Schedules posted one month out. Required in-person training Mon–Fri, 9:30 AM – 6 PM.
3 Motivation & Intent ▼
What you're looking for:
  • Are they commission-driven or comfort-driven?
  • Do they talk about growth, or just money?
  • Do they understand what retail sales actually is?
Complete at least two fields.
4 Sales Mindset & Coachability ▼
Goal: Prevent an ego hire. We want people who can conform to a process — uniformity, structure, T-charting, sales scripts — instead of "winging it." Complete at least two fields.
Xfinity sales scenarios — listen for how they think on their feet.
5 Work Ethic & Accountability ▼
Complete at least two fields.
6 Role Reality Check ▼
We work hard to play hard. Weekly contests for free lunches, big prizes (TVs, speakers, concert/sporting tickets), and a four-day, three-night all-expense-paid Winner's Circle trip for top performers. Which of these would peak the candidate's interest?
Discuss with the candidate:
  1. Full benefits package (Health, Dental, Vision, 401k with 3% match) via ADP if selected.
  2. Hourly + commission job. Hourly rate is ____ plus commission. Most reps make $18–$23/hr; top performers earn over $30/hr.
  3. Bi-weekly pay. First check of each month = hourly only. Last check = hourly + commission for that month.
  4. Commissions on a two-month delay (April sales paid in June, etc). 30-day money-back guarantee on customer side; commissions paid after the 30-day audit. No chargebacks after payout.
7 Should We Proceed? ▼
Ideal candidate looks like:
  • Coachability
  • Sales potential
  • Energy level
  • Communication skills
  • Culture fit
8 Upload Candidate's Resume / Photos ▼
Snap photos or upload a PDF of the candidate's resume so the hiring team can review everything before they come in. You can upload up to 4 files. Optional — but strongly encouraged.
